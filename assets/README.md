# Phoxal Kinetic identity

The full PHOXAL wordmark is the primary identity for the website, documentation and terminal headers.
The articulated X is the companion mark for application icons and favicons.
Use the wordmark at its original aspect ratio and preserve the X's two circular pivots.
Keep the icon's rounded graphite tile and transparent outer margin; do not put it on a second square background or mask it a second time.

## Files

- `phoxal-wordmark.png`: offwhite and teal wordmark on graphite, 2172 by 724 pixels.
- `phoxal-icon.png`: master rounded application icon with transparency, 1254 by 1254 pixels.
- `favicon.png`: 64-pixel icon derivative.
- `apple-touch-icon.png`: 180-pixel icon derivative.
- `phoxal-social-kinetic.png`: social preview, 1200 by 630 pixels.

The simulator embeds a 512-pixel derivative of the application icon.
Applications carry their own assets and never load them from this website at runtime.
The terminal renders a five-line text adaptation of PHOXAL with an accented X, or compact PHOXAL lettering at narrow widths.
It retains the terminal's own text/background colors and honors NO_COLOR.

## Artwork provenance

The maintainer selected the Kinetic direction from three original concepts.
The final artwork was created with the built-in image-generation tool using that selected concept as its reference.
Pixel-size derivatives were exported with macOS sips.
Rejected transparent wordmark generations are not product assets.

### Wordmark prompt

> Make a clean final PHOXAL wordmark from the 01 Kinetic concept in the LEFT column only.
> Exact letters PHOXAL.
> Bold technical sans lettering in flat offwhite #EDF4F2, same distinctive X whose bottom-left to top-right diagonal is a teal mechanical connecting link with circular pivot holes at both ends, teal #20C9B7.
> One single centered horizontal wordmark on a perfectly uniform solid dark background EXACTLY #11181B.
> Wide 3:1 canvas.
> Logo fills 90% of canvas width.
> No labels, no standalone app icon, no other text.
> Flat professional logo art, clean solid filled shapes, no gradients, no texture, no noise or speckles, no outlines, no glow, no shadows.
> Preserve the first reference's typography and readable spacing.
> This is a final website header logo asset, not a presentation board.

### Application icon prompt

> Create a production app icon based EXACTLY on the small app icon under 01 Kinetic in the LEFT reference column.
> One square image, a dark graphite #1A252A rounded-square tile centered occupying 82% of canvas, truly TRANSPARENT outer margin, smooth generously rounded corners.
> Inside: a bold teal #20C9B7 mechanical X, made of a diagonal link rising from bottom left to top right with one circular hole near each round end, and a second teal diagonal behind it running top left to bottom right with a small dark separation at the crossing.
> Preserve this X geometry from the leftmost reference icon.
> Simple crisp flat vector-like artwork.
> No lettering whatsoever.
> No shadow, glow, gradients, speckles, grit, texture or decorative details.
> Very clean opaque tile with smooth sharp alpha edge outside the rounded rectangle.
> Do not include the wordmark or any of the other concepts.
